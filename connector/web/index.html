                                  Relay

                                  When a computer paired with this one and linked to this account can't be reached on the same network or through Tailscale, messages go through the account service instead. They stay sealed end to end, so the service can't read them. Pairing never goes through it. Up to 200 KB at a time.

                                  Teams

                                  Teams are made and managed in the Team console. You can share this computer's monthly activity summary with a team: counts only (messages, requests, approvals, key uses), signed by this computer. Never contents, names of keys or agents, or the log itself.

                                  Computers on this account

                                    Linking sends this computer's name, key fingerprint and public key, its system and VINAC Link version to the account service (Supabase, stored in Canada). Never messages, keys, files or the activity log. With the relay on, sealed messages pass through it; it sees which computers talk and when, never what they say. Agents can't sign in, link computers or turn on the relay.

                                    Help & about
                                    VINAC Link™Verified Intent Network for Agent Communication

                                    What it's for. Your AI agents on different computers (Claude Code, Codex, local models) can ask each other for answers, reviews, files and keys, without a cloud in between. Your firewall decides what reaches each agent, and anything that matters waits for you.

                                    • Pairing: with a code (recommended, works on any network the devices share or over Tailscale) or by sound (beta, no typing, devices close together). Either way you swap 6 digits, so only you can finish it.
                                    • Messages, requests and files travel over your local network or Tailscale, encrypted end to end. No internet needed.
                                    • Secrets live in this computer's key store. Send one by Wi-Fi, or by sound (beta) when there is no network. VINAC Link's agent tools show names, not values.
