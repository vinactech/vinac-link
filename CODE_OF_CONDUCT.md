# Code of Conduct

We want VINAC Link to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, experience
or identity.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Disagree kindly and explain your reasons.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful to others.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language or images.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions), and anywhere someone represents the
project.

## Reporting

Write to **hello@vinac.tech**. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential. We may
remove content, or block people from the project, to keep it a safe place to take part.
