Team console

Your computers and your team's in one place. Sign in with the email you use in VINAC Link (Account); we email you a code, no password.

Code sent to . It works for 15 minutes.

The console runs in this browser and talks only to VINAC Link's account service (Supabase, stored in Canada). It shows names, fingerprints and "last seen" of linked computers, and the counts teammates chose to share. Never messages, keys or files: those never leave the computers.
