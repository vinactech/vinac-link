Roadmap

From a first experiment to agents you can trust.

VINAC Link started in July 2026 with one question: why can't the AI agents on my own computers help each other, safely? Here is how far we've come, what we're building now, and where we're going.

  • Done
  • Now
  • Next
  • Later
  1. July 2026

    The question

    Our agents worked on several computers at once, some of them offline on purpose. Every time one needed a key or an answer from another, a person carried it across by hand. We started looking for a better way.

  2. Summer 2026

    Two computers meet

    The first prototype: two computers find each other with a short burst of sound or a typed code, a person confirms a code on both screens, and their agents trade encrypted messages. No account, no cloud, no setup.

  3. October 2026

    VINAC Link becomes a product

    The prototype grows into a product of its own, built around a firewall the person controls.

    • Intent firewall
    • Keys and tokens without exposure
    • Encrypted files
    • Claude Code, Codex, OpenClaw
  4. November 2026 We are here

    Early access

    Published, well-studied cryptography for every connection, full offline use, a vault for your keys, installers that include everything, and this website. VINAC Link is free to download and use today. Pairing and moving keys by sound work, and stay in beta while we test them on more hardware.

    • Noise protocol
    • Pair by picking the other computer
    • Invites
    • Ask for a key in one click
    • Secrets vault with a passphrase lock
    • Restricted / Standard / Auto modes
    • Messages wait for offline devices
    • Offline over Wi-Fi, hotspot or cable
    • Local models
    • Installers for Windows, macOS and Linux
    • Mac app notarized by Apple
    • Sound pairing (beta)
  5. Next

    Simpler and proven

    • Project hand-off. Start a project on one computer and continue on another: VINAC Link reads the project's .env.example, shows which keys are missing, and moves them with one approval. The build gets them when it runs; the agent never sees them.
    • Independent security audit. We will publish the results in full.
    • A signed Windows installer, without "unknown publisher" warnings. The Mac app is already signed and notarized by Apple.
    • Relay (planned). Reach your computers across different networks, or when one is asleep. Messages are sealed before they leave your computer; the relay only passes them on and can't read them. Pairing still happens in person. Tell me when it's ready →
    • Approvals from your phone (planned). Approve or reject a waiting request with a passkey, never by replying in a chat.
  6. Later

    For teams and organisations

    • Teams: shared firewall rules, audit export, single sign-on and an overview of every device.
    • Organisations: on-premises and air-gapped setups for labs and regulated networks. Talk to us →
    • Delivery by USB stick for machines that are never online at the same time.
    • Introductions: a device you already trust introduces two others, and you still confirm with a code.
    • Works with A2A: connect VINAC Link to the open agent-to-agent standard, with your firewall still checking every request.
    • Password and secrets managers: agents use keys kept in the manager you already trust, still without seeing them.
Our vision

Every agent you run, on every computer you own, working together, with a person deciding what's allowed.

AI agents will do more of our work every year, on more machines. We want the link between them to be as simple as plugging in a cable, and as safe as the person in charge decides.

Trust you can see

Every request states its intent, every permission is visible, and nothing moves that you didn't allow. Agents use trust; only people create it.

Works anywhere

At a desk, on the move, or in a room with no internet at all. The same link, the same rules, online or offline.

Open by default

Open source, published cryptography and an open list of intents, so any agent or app can join and anyone can check how it works.

What stays free

Everything VINAC Link does on your own computers is free and stays free: pairing, encryption, the firewall, keys, files, offline use and every agent plugin. The relay and the team features run on servers or serve whole organisations, so they will be paid options. We'll say what they cost before they leave beta.
