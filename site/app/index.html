              Send

              Goes through your account, sealed for that computer only. It's kept there as a key from this browser.

              1. On the computer: Secrets › Receive… › From your phone.
              2. Hold the phone close to it, volume up, and press Send encrypted key by sound. It takes 10–20 seconds.

              Sound is in beta. A nearby microphone can record the tones; they open only on the device it's sealed for, but until the security review use test keys.

              Open this code on the other phone: its camera, or Receive in its app. It opens only there, once.

              Pair another phone

              1. On the other phone, open this code (its camera, or Receive in its app). It works for 10 minutes.

              2. It then shows its own code: open it here with Scan, or with this phone's camera. Or let it play its sound while this phone listens.
