Your keys, on your phone

Keep API keys and tokens here, encrypted with a passphrase only you know, and hand them to your computer or another phone, by code or by sound. Nothing goes to any server.

If you forget the passphrase, the keys here can't be opened. Keep copies of important keys elsewhere.
